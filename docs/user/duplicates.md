# Duplicate Recruit Warning

When you add a new recruit, Hoop Harbor checks whether she might already be in the database. If she might be, it shows a warning **before** saving, so the staff doesn't end up with two partial records for the same player.

## When the warning appears

The warning appears when you click **Save** on a **new** recruit and someone already in the database has:

- the **same last name**, **and**
- the **same graduation year**, **and**
- at least one of these:
  - a **similar first name** (for example, *Jordan* and *Jordyn*, or a matching preferred name like *Kate* for *Katherine*)
  - the **same high school**
  - the **same club or travel team**

Capital letters, accents, and apostrophes don't matter (*López* matches *Lopez*, *O'Neil* matches *ONeil*). "HS" and "High School" count as the same.

Editing an existing recruit never shows this warning.

## What to do

The warning lists each possible match with her name, class year, and school. Choose one:

| Button | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Open existing** | Opens that recruit's profile. **Nothing new is created.** | It's the same player. Add your information to her existing profile instead. |
| **Go back** | Closes the warning. Everything you typed stays in the form. | You want to double-check or correct a field first. |
| **Create anyway** | Saves the new recruit. | It's a different player who happens to look similar, such as twins, sisters, or a common name. |

You can also press **Esc** to close the warning, which works the same as **Go back**.

## Why it doesn't block you

Twins, sisters, and common names are real, so the warning only asks. You always have the final say.

## Examples

| You're adding | Already in the database | Warning? |
|---|---|---|
| Jordan Smith, 2028, Westfield High School | Jordan Smith, 2028, Westfield High School | Yes, likely the same player |
| Jordyn Smith, 2028, Westfield High School | Jordan Smith, 2028, Westfield High School | Yes, similar name and same school |
| Jordan Smith, 2030, Lincoln High School | Jordan Smith, 2028, Westfield High School | No, different class year |
| Taylor Smith, 2028, Lincoln High School | Jordan Smith, 2028, Westfield High School | No, nothing in common but the last name |
